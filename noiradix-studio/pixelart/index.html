About NOIRADIX PixelArt

NOIRADIX PixelArt
High-Performance Multi-Layer Pixel Art Editor
v2.0.0 Stable PIX2 v1.1 Compatible

NOIRADIX PixelArt 2.0 introduces structural layers, named regions, semantic isolation, and unified selection scaling & rotation for the PIX2 1.1 graphics standard.

Standard Format: PIX2 1.1 Structural Graphics
