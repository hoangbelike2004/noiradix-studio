# 💖 Donate & Support NOIRADIX Studio

Thank you for considering supporting **NOIRADIX Studio**!

NOIRADIX Studio is an open-source, independent developer initiative dedicated to creating the most efficient, AI-native pixel art and spritesheet standard (`.pix2`) and developer toolsuite for 2D game creators worldwide.

Your sponsorship helps:
* Fund ongoing development and maintenance.
* Expand engine importers (Godot 4, Unity, Phaser, Defold, Unreal).
* Keep the tool 100% free, pure vanilla, and open source with zero telemetry or paywalls.
